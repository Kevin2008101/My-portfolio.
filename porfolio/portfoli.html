About

I’m Kevin Nnorom. I’m a web developer and founder of KEVTECH. I work on the front end and back end of websites and web apps. I build them, and they go into my portfolio for others to see. But the websites and web apps that I build are for real businesses and they get put into use.

My most recent build was Itán, a restaurant website for a modern West African kitchen in Lagos. It has a cinematic hero, a filterable menu, an order cart that sends orders through WhatsApp, and a reservation form. A polished, customer-facing site that I’ve enjoyed building.

I care about clean code, fast loading times, and rendering well on low-end Android Devices as well as on wide screen monitors.
